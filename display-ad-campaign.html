Display Ad Campaign

For this assignment, we developed a complete display advertising campaign for Cedar Pointe Golf Course called Nine & Wine. Our campaign promoted a monthly women's night out that combines a nine-hole round of golf with wine, food, and live patio entertainment. Our primary goal was to generate revenue by selling all 72 available event spots at $24 per ticket, with an optional $15 golf cart rental. We also wanted to increase brand awareness among potential customers in Ames and the Des Moines area.

We identified our target audience as women ages 21–45 within approximately 40 miles of Boone, including college students, young professionals, and women looking for a fun night out with friends. We developed a campaign strategy using Facebook, Instagram, and Google Search, with a four-week advertising period and increased spending during the final ten days before the event. We also included retargeting to reach people who visited the ticket page but did not purchase. Our strategy included researching industry benchmarks and establishing measurements such as tickets sold, cost per ticket, click-through rate, and return on ad spend.

For the creative portion, we designed four versions of the same advertisement in different sizes to fit various website placements. Each ad maintained consistent branding through Cedar Pointe's logo, maroon and cream colors, golf course imagery, and the Nine & Wine messaging. We used the slogan "Your Night Out. Your Kind of Fun." to communicate the event's relaxed, social atmosphere and included a call to action encouraging visitors to join us.

Finally, we developed a three-page GitHub website to showcase our work. The home page provides an overview of our semester-long project and summaries of our assignments. The ads page explains our campaign strategy and displays the four advertisement variations, with each ad linking directly to our landing page. The landing page provides visitors with the event details and a clear path to learn more and reserve their spot.

Overall, we combined campaign planning, audience targeting, advertisement design, and website development to create a cohesive advertising campaign that promotes Cedar Pointe's Nine & Wine event.
