Before You Head Out

What does the golf price include?

The $24 per-person price covers the nine-hole greens fee. Cart rental is an additional $15 per rider. Wine and pizza are sold separately.

How many people can I book for?

You can book for one to four golfers using the reservation form.

How late can we golf?

Tee times run from 4–6:30 p.m., and play is limited by available daylight. Food, wine, and live patio entertainment continue until 10 p.m.

Do I need to reserve a cart?

Please request a cart when you check in. Rental is $15 per rider, with a maximum of two riders per cart.

When do I pay?

Please pay in the clubhouse when you check in for your tee time. No online payment is required.

Who should I contact with questions?

Contact Cedar Pointe Golf Course at (515) 432-6002.
