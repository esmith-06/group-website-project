Three Ways We'll Grow the Nine & Wine List

Each customer group gets a clear reason to join today and a promise worth staying subscribed for.

1

Nine & Wine Ticket Buyers

Where/how: This customer group will find an opt-in checkbox at the online ticket checkout through our landing page, which will be left unchecked by default. For those who are purchasing tickets in person, there will be a QR code on table tents that says "Scan for 10% off your first purchase." We will reach these customers through Meta and Google ads that lead them to our landing page and the checkout page.

Immediate value: The online and in-person customers will be offered a 10% discount on their first purchase of a Nine & Wine event ticket.

Long-term value: By joining our email list, customers will receive first access to future Nine & Wine event details before they go public. Additionally, as an email list member, if they attend eight Nine & Wine events, they receive a free round of four on their ninth attended event.

2

Iowa State Students & Young Professionals (Ames/DSM)

Where/how: Flyers with QR codes will be posted around campus and Campustown businesses, as well as in downtown Des Moines. Cedar Pointe will have an Instagram giveaway where those interested have to tag their friends and join the email list to enter to win a free round for four.

Immediate value: Young adults in the Ames/DSM area will receive the same 10% discount off their first event ticket purchase as seen above. Students in the Ames/DSM area will receive a special 15% student discount.

Long-term value: This group will receive student-only deals and will also be eligible for the "Get your ninth event free" deal.

3

Current Cedar Pointe Golfers & Pizza Customers (Boone)

Where/how: Cedar Pointe employees will ask customers if they would like to join the email list for exclusive event updates. The pizza shop will print a QR code on pizza boxes and receipts that links to an event-update email sign-up.

Immediate value: Existing golfers will receive the same offer of 10% off their first event ticket purchase. Pizza customers will receive an offer for $5 off their next pizza.

Long-term value: Existing customers will be entered into the "9th round free" deal and a loyalty program where benefits (like a free birthday round) are rewarded. They'll also be the first to receive updates on all Cedar Pointe events.
