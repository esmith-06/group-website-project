How We’ll Build the List

Three distinct customer groups and the value we offer each one.

Group 1: Nine & Wine Ticket Buyers

Where & How We Collect Emails: Online ticket buyers can subscribe through an optional, unchecked-by-default checkbox at checkout. In-person buyers can scan QR codes on table tents promoting “10% off your first purchase.” Meta and Google ads direct customers to the event landing page and checkout.

Immediate Value: 10% off their first Nine & Wine event ticket purchase.

Long-Term Value: Early access to upcoming Nine & Wine event announcements. After attending eight events, subscribers receive a free round of golf for four on their ninth event.

Group 2: Iowa State Students & Young Professionals

Where & How We Collect Emails: QR-code flyers around Iowa State's campus, Campustown businesses, and downtown Des Moines. An Instagram giveaway asks participants to tag friends and explicitly opt in to the email list for a chance to win a free round of golf for four.

Immediate Value: Young professionals receive 10% off their first event ticket; eligible students receive a 15% student discount.

Long-Term Value: Exclusive student-only deals and eligibility for the ninth-event-free loyalty incentive.

Group 3: Cedar Pointe Golfers & Boone Pizza Customers

Where & How We Collect Emails: Cedar Pointe employees invite existing customers to subscribe for event updates. The partnering pizza business places QR codes on pizza boxes and receipts linking to the email sign-up page.

Immediate Value: Existing golfers receive 10% off their first Nine & Wine event ticket. Pizza customers receive $5 off their next pizza purchase.

Long-Term Value: Access to the ninth-event-free loyalty incentive, additional rewards such as a complimentary birthday round, and early updates about all Cedar Pointe events.
