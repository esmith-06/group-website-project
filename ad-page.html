Ad Strategy Overview

Nine & Wine is a one-night event at Cedar Pointe Golf Course in Boone that combines a relaxed nine-hole round with wine tastings from a mobile wine truck, pizza from Cedar Pointe’s on-site kitchen, and live music. Our primary goal is to drive revenue by selling out the event’s 72 golfer spots at $24 per ticket, with an optional $15 cart rental. Our secondary goal is to build brand awareness in Ames and Des Moines, where many potential customers have never visited Cedar Pointe or still think of it as a members-only country club. The campaign will run for four weeks before the event on Facebook, Instagram, and Google Search. We will spend more heavily in the final ten days, when urgency is highest, and retarget people who visited the ticket page without buying. We will measure success by tickets sold, cost per ticket, click-through rate, return on ad spend, and the share of guests from Ames and Des Moines ZIP codes, collected at checkout.

Target Audience

Our audience is women ages 21 to 45 within about 40 miles of Boone, focused on Ames, Ankeny, and the Des Moines metro. We set the minimum age at 21 because the event includes wine tastings. Within this group we are speaking to two segments: women in their twenties, including Iowa State students, recent grads, and young professionals looking for a new girls’ night out, and women in their thirties and forties looking for a fun break, a birthday outing, or a night with friends. Most are casual players or have never golfed at all. Industry data shows this group is ready to be reached. According to the National Golf Foundation, the number of on-course female golfers in the U.S. rose 45 percent from 2020 to 2025, and women now make up a record-high 28% of on-course players, with even larger shares among beginners and off-course players. The NGF also reports that roughly two-thirds of new on-course golfers first played off-course, at places like Topgolf, which is exactly the experience Nine & Wine builds on. On Facebook and Instagram, we will target interests such as wine, wine tasting, live music, girls’ night, local events, and Topgolf. On Google, we will bid on searches like “things to do in Des Moines this weekend,” “wine tasting near me,” and “girls night ideas.”

Design / Hook

Our hook is “Your night out. Your kind of fun.” The biggest barrier for our audience is the belief that golf is stuffy, hard, or only for experienced players. The hook answers that by making the event about the guest, not her golf skills. It puts the night out first and lets her define what fun looks like, whether that’s a great shot, a new favorite wine, or laughing with friends at sunset. Our visuals will show groups of women laughing on the green with wine glasses in hand, golden-hour lighting, live music, and pizza, with the golf playful rather than competitive. Short vertical videos for Instagram Reels and Stories will lead the campaign. Supporting copy will emphasize that no experience or membership is needed, that the format is beginner-friendly, and that Cedar Pointe is an easy drive from Ames and Des Moines. Every ad will end with a clear call to action: “Grab your friends and get your tickets.”

Financial Estimates

We based our projections on WordStream by LocaliQ’s 2026 benchmarks for the Sports & Recreation industry. On Facebook, Sports & Recreation traffic campaigns average a 1.77% click-through rate and a $0.59 cost per click, and lead campaigns in the same category convert at an average rate of 6.75%, which we used to estimate how many clicks become ticket sales. For Google Search, Sports & Recreation ads average an 8.75% click-through rate, a $2.77 cost per click, and a 7.69% conversion rate. With a total budget of $800, we plan to spend $600 on Facebook and Instagram, which should produce about 57,000 impressions, 1,017 clicks, and 69 ticket sales. The remaining $200 goes to Google Search for about 72 clicks and 6 ticket sales. That’s about 74 projected sales, enough to sell out all 72 spots at an average cost of about $11 per ticket. A sold-out event brings in $1,728 in ticket revenue. If half of the guests rent a cart, that adds $540, for a total of $2,268 and a return of about 2.8 times the ad spend. Pizza sales and the awareness gained among people who see the ads but don’t attend would add value beyond these figures.
